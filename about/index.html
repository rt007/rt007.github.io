Hello, I'm Romal, a tech researcher with an IT and business management background.

Based in Dublin, I explore the intersection of emerging technologies and real-world systems—from digital twins and smart cities to AI-driven tools that make complex information tangible and useful.

Professional Background

2024 — Present

Researcher & Project Lead

Insight Research Ireland Centre for Data Analytics

"The best way to predict the future is to invent it." - Alan Kay

My interest in technology has always been grounded in a practical question: how do systems work, and how can they work better? That curiosity led me from a foundation in IT engg. and master's in business management into the world of research—exploring how digital infrastructure, data, and emerging technologies shape the way cities and organisations function.

Today, my work sits at the intersection of digital twin technology, smart city systems, and AI. I collaborate with institutions and public bodies to translate complex technical concepts into research that has real-world impact—work that bridges the gap between what's technically possible and what's practically useful.

Beyond research, I build tools and projects driven by the same principle: taking something complex and making it accessible. Whether that's an AI-powered app, an immersive 360° environment, or a podcast on emerging technologies, I'm driven by the idea that technology should be something people can understand, engage with, and ultimately benefit from.
